# Survey of LEO handover and pass-window prediction papers

Section 2 of the paper states that twenty LEO handover and pass-window prediction papers
published since 2024 report accuracy or F1, lead time, handover latency, packet delivery
ratio, ping-pong rate and throughput, and that none reports inference energy, memory, tail
latency, dollar cost or carbon. surveyed_papers.csv lists the twenty papers with what each
reports, so the claim can be checked paper by paper. Every DOI or arXiv identifier in the
list is checked against its registry record.
